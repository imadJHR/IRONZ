# Black Friday product images

Tool: built-in `image_gen`, image edit mode. The six original posters remain in `public/black-friday/`. The supplied white and pale-yellow IRONZ background is preserved as `public/black-friday/ironz-background.png`.

The edited product photos are 1254 × 1254 pixels. The selected outputs were encoded as WebP at quality 85 with Sharp; no further creative edits were made during encoding. Next/Image serves responsive sizes at configured quality 60.

## Prompt template used for each image

Product-photo edit and compositing. Image 1 is the target source photograph embedded in an advertising poster. Image 2 is the EXACT user-supplied background to use. Create a square 1:1 ecommerce product bundle photograph: isolate exactly these products from image 1: [PRODUCTS]. Preserve original package logos, correct product names, colors, labels, flavours, proportions and bottle shapes. Remove all outer advertising text, prices, store shelves, footer, tiny price stickers and icons. Place the actual products together, centered and large in the foreground on the exact white background with pale-yellow IRONZ MUSCLE logo from image 2. Preserve the logo artwork behind the products; it may be partly occluded by products but do not redraw/reinvent it. Products fill about 78% of frame width, all fully visible with 8% margin. Soft realistic contact shadows, premium neutral studio lighting and sharp readable package labels. Do not add products or duplicate containers. The Fitness File, if present, is just a yellow paper booklet with a printed image of a body scanner, do not add a real body scanner. No extra overlay text, no promotional copy, no badges, no prices. Consistent camera front view and even white lighting for an entire six-image catalogue series.
